# Mini_project-Surge-protection-circuit
A mini-project implementing a surge protection circuit designed to protect electronic devices from voltage spikes and transient surges. The project focuses on circuit design, component selection, and safe voltage regulation for improved electronic system protection.
Surge Protection Circuit 

**1. Project Overview** 

This project involves the design and implementation of a surge protection circuit for protecting electronic systems from transient voltage spikes and electrical surges. The circuit is designed to limit excessive voltage and protect connected components from potential damage. 

**2. Objectives**

.Design a circuit capable of suppressing transient voltage surges.

.Protect sensitive electronic components from excessive voltage.

.Select suitable protection components based on circuit requirements. 

.Simulate and verify the circuit operation. 

.Develop a compact and reliable protection solution. 

**3. Key Features**

**5. Working Principle**

The surge protection circuit monitors the voltage supplied to the connected load. During normal operating conditions, the circuit allows the required voltage to reach the load. When a transient voltage spike occurs, the relay reacts to it and mechanically diverts the excessive voltage to the MOV varistor and as the excessive current passes through the output side of the circuit the fuse breaks , thereby reducing the stress on the connected electronic components.
